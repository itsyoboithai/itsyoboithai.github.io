How the record works

The goal is simple: 600 people eat tire sur la neige in the same place, at the same time. Final rules will be confirmed with the record adjudicator before the event.

  1. Register

    Sign up below. Every participant needs a name on the list.

  2. Get counted

    Check in on the day and get a wristband. Independent witnesses count each person.

  3. Get your taffy

    Hot maple syrup goes on the snow. Roll it onto your stick.

  4. Eat together

    On the whistle, everyone eats at once. Stay in your spot until the count is done.

FAQ

What is tire sur la neige?

It's a Quebec winter treat. Hot maple syrup is poured on clean snow, where it cools into taffy, and you roll it up on a stick.

Do I need to be a McGill student?

No. Students, staff, alumni and guests are all welcome, as long as you register.

What if there's no snow?

We'll bring it. If needed, we'll use snow or crushed ice trucked in for the event.

Is it free?

Yes. Registration and taffy are free for all participants.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

Tire sur la neige is made from maple syrup only. Tell us about any needs on the day and our team will help.

Can I bring friends who haven't registered?

They can watch and cheer, but only registered people in the counted zone count toward the record.

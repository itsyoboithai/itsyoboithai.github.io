Style It Yourself

Lead on product, design and backend. January 2026. Open source.

A wardrobe app that doesn’t just say “this outfit works.” It tells you why. I led product, design and the backend, then open-sourced it under AGPL-3.0 as the base for a bigger consumer product.

PythonFastAPIReactPostgreSQLSupabaseGemini API
The idea
Outfit apps suggest looks but never explain them, so people don’t trust them.
How it works
A FastAPI backend with 20+ endpoints covers closet management, outfit checks, AI recommendations and AI try-on.
The fun part
A deterministic score built from color harmony (HSL hue distance), formality, nearest-match colors and pairing rules, with a plain-language reason for every mismatch.
